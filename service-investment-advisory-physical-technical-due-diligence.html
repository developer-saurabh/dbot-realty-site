What we do

Scope

  • Architectural and structural inspection
  • MEP design, execution and façade review
  • Statutory and compliance validation
  • Schedule, budget and costing review
  • CAPEX, OPEX and repair forecasts
  • ESG review of the operating asset
Deliverables

What you receive

  • Technical due diligence report
  • Defects and risk register
  • Cost-to-rectify and CAPEX forecast
Standards used
  • IFC Performance Standards (where ESG review applies)
  • RICS Red Book, IVS and IBBI for valuation inputs
  • Equator Principles and lender policy alignment
